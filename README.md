# Automotive Clips

Starklips Otomotiv manufactures and supplies automotive clips, retainers and fastening components for OEM and aftermarket applications.

## Product Types

- Push-type clips
- Trim panel clips
- Door panel clips
- Bumper clips
- Fender clips
- Interior trim retainers
- Plastic fasteners
- Metal fastening components
- Automotive retainers
- Assembly clips

## Applications

Our automotive clips and fastening products are used in:

- Passenger cars
- Light commercial vehicles
- Trucks and buses
- Interior trim systems
- Exterior trim systems
- Bumpers and body panels
- Door and panel assemblies
- General automotive assembly applications
